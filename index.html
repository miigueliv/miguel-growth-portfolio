A little about me

Marketing brain.
Curious human.

I’m Miguel, a Portuguese marketer based in Amsterdam. My path into growth has taken me through tourism, sales, startups and marketing — which means I’m interested not just in campaigns, but in people, markets and what makes someone take action.

I like getting close to the work: researching an audience, sharpening the message, building the campaign, looking at the numbers and improving what happens next. I’m at my best where strategy meets execution.

Outside work, you’ll usually find me planning a trip, surfing, running, at the gym, exploring a new city or getting lost in a good book.

How I work

Clear thinking.
Useful execution.

02 / BUILD

Make the message matter

Connect positioning, creative, outbound and landing pages into a coherent journey rather than a pile of disconnected tactics.

03 / IMPROVE

Measure what counts

Look beyond vanity metrics. Learn from conversion, lead quality and pipeline outcomes, then improve the next iteration.
